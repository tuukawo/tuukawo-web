Account deletion

Delete your Tuukawo account

The quickest way is in the app: open Account, then Delete my account. If you cannot use the app, send us this form. Our staff will call the phone number to confirm it is really you, then delete the account.

What we delete and what we keep

  • Deleted: your name, phone number, email, saved places, photos, ID documents, next of kin and signatures.
  • Kept without your name or contact details: payment and trip records, because the business must keep financial records.
  • We cannot delete an account while a trip is in progress or platform fees are unpaid. We will tell you if that is the case.
